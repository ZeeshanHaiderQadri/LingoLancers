# Lingo Product Context

## Register

product

## Product purpose

Lingo is a voice-first Agentic Business Desk for export, ecommerce, and services SMEs. It turns a business goal into an evidence-backed mission with reviewed assets, approval gates, and a measurable outcome.

## Primary users

- SME founders who need a clear launch or growth plan without managing multiple AI tools.
- Team reviewers who approve market claims, spend, publishing, and generated assets.
- Operators who need visibility into agent work, sources, failures, and cost.

## Product principles

- Show progress and evidence, never simulated agent thought.
- Use plain, calm language and make approvals explicit.
- Keep the default workflow small, bounded, and cost-aware.
- Treat external actions as human-approved operations.

## Anti-references

- A generic grid of disconnected AI tools.
- A decorative agent animation with no traceable work.
- An overloaded dashboard that hides what needs approval.
